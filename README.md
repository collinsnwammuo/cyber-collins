## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a Computer Engineering graduate transitioning into cybersecurity with a focus on SOC operations and blue team work. My engineering background gives me a strong foundation in how networks and systems actually work at a low level, which I am now applying to threat detection, packet analysis, and incident response.

I do not just follow tutorials. I build hands-on labs, break things, fix them, and document everything. My [Wireshark portfolio](https://github.com/collinsnwammuo/Wireshark-projects) is a series of practical exercises I built from scratch on Kali Linux and Windows 10 VMs -- covering everything from TCP handshake analysis to live ARP spoofing attacks and real malware PCAP investigations using samples from malware-traffic-analysis.net.

I am currently working toward my first SOC analyst role and actively building the skills and evidence to back that up.
